## 🎯 Team Vision

> _"Bridging the gap between students and their campus — one navigation step at a time."_

Team **Velmora** is dedicated to creating smart, accessible, and life-saving campus technology. Our goal with **WayFindYou** is to make every corner of the campus — every lab, classroom, restroom, and emergency exit — instantly findable by any student, anywhere, anytime.
